The operational challenge

Keep the plan, the field work,
and the record connected.

Get the right person to the job.

Skills, certifications, time off, and competing appointments all shape a dispatch decision.

Know when work needs attention.

Required service steps and visible deadlines help technicians and supervisors act on the same information.

Keep the reasons behind the result.

Recorded interventions and event history make an incident easier to examine after the work is done.

What the platform does

Clarity at every handoff.

Purpose-built workspaces connect dispatch, field execution, and operational review.

Qualified dispatch
Review availability and qualification constraints. Compare baseline or OR-Tools scheduling recommendations before confirming an appointment.
Guided execution
Follow an eight-stage HVAC workflow with required answers, conditional fields, and recorded service evidence.
SLA supervision
See at-risk work and retained alerts. Acknowledge, pause, resume, or override eligible stages with a recorded reason.
Operational visibility
Review completion, duration, SLA, and utilization metrics with definitions and warehouse freshness in view.
Job context
Keep the customer name, service address, category, priority, appointment, and workflow together in the work order.
Audit and replay
Reconstruct the event timeline and inspect delivery evidence without re-executing business commands.

How it works

One job. A clear path forward.

The web portal coordinates the work. The technician app carries the service workflow into the field.

  1. 01

    Record the request

    Create a work order with the service location, category, and priority.

    Operations portal
  2. 02

    Qualify and schedule

    Check technician fit and availability, then confirm the appointment.

    Dispatcher
  3. 03

    Execute and supervise

    Complete the guided stages. Escalate work that needs a supervisor decision.

    Technician + supervisor
  4. 04

    Complete and review

    Retain the service record, review performance, and inspect event history.

    Operations + analyst
